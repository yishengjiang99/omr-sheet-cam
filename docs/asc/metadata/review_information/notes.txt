REGARDING GUIDELINE 4.3(a) - WHY THIS APP IS A UNIQUE PRODUCT

Sheet Music Reader (home-screen name: SheetCam) is an original, single-purpose app built around a specialized on-device optical music recognition (OMR) pipeline. It is not a template, a reskin, or a repackaging of any other app, and it does not duplicate any other app we publish.

What it does that is different: point the camera at a printed page of sheet music and the app reads the notation itself. A segmentation model (SegNet) locates the staff regions, the page geometry is corrected and dewarped, and a transformer encoder-decoder transcribes the notation staff by staff, including multi-system grand-staff pages. The output is structured music data (a MIDI file), played back with note-by-note highlighting synchronized on the original page image. A demo video is attached to these review notes: a scanned score playing back while each recognized note highlights in time.

Original technology and code: all recognition runs on the device (ONNX Runtime with Core ML acceleration); score photos are never uploaded for recognition. The models are adapted from homr, an open-source research project, credited in the app's licenses. Everything else - camera capture, page processing, decoding orchestration, MIDI generation, playback, and the interface - is an original native Swift codebase written only for this product.

Not a duplicate of our other apps: our audio transcription app (AI Music Radar) does the opposite job - it turns recorded sound into notation. Music Reader starts from a printed page; Radar starts from audio. They share no recognition code, no models, and no workflow, and neither can perform the other's function. Generic document scanner apps do not read music notation at all.

HOW TO REVIEW
No account or login is required. On the Library home screen (shown while the Library is empty) tap "Try sample picture" to scan a bundled sample page - no camera or printed music needed. For further scans: back to Library, tap "Camera", then "Try sample picture" at the bottom, or tap "Photos" and import any image of sheet music. Open a saved scan to play it; tap any highlighted note to play from there.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
UIBackgroundModes: audio is used only so playback of a recognized score continues on the lock screen and in the background, with Now Playing controls (play, pause, previous, next, seek) like a music player. The mode is active only during playback. The app records no audio and uses no microphone.

PRIVACY
All recognition is on-device; photos and scores are never uploaded. The app sends only anonymous usage events (no photos, MIDI, email, or location), and no data is used for tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
