In-app purchase: this version adds an optional auto-renewable subscription, Music Reader Pro (group "Music Reader Pro"): com.ragnus.vp.pro.monthly ($4.99/month) and com.ragnus.vp.pro.yearly ($29.99/year). Pro removes the free limit of 5 page scans per day; everything else (on-device recognition, player, instruments) is the same for free users. No account or login is required.

How to reach the paywall (no camera, photo or sheet music needed): the free tier allows 5 page scans per day; the 6th scan of the day opens the "Music Reader Pro" paywall with the Yearly and Monthly plans, Subscribe buttons, Restore purchases, and Terms of Use / Privacy Policy links.
1. Fresh install, Library home screen: tap "Try sample picture". This button is shown only while the Library is empty, so it is there for the first scan only.
2. For every further scan: go back (top-left) to the Library home screen, tap "Camera", then tap "Try sample picture" at the bottom of the Camera screen (it scans a bundled sample page, no photo is taken). Repeat. Alternatively tap "Photos" on the Library home screen and import any image.
3. The 6th scan of the day shows the paywall.

Purchases work in the sandbox environment with a sandbox Apple ID (Subscribe completes and unlocks unlimited scans; Restore purchases restores it). All music recognition runs on the device.
