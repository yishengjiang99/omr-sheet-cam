AI Camera - Music Reader Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-26 · App: AI Camera - Music Reader (iOS and iPadOS) · Operator: GrepAwk LLC

Summary

The app does not collect personal data. Sheet-music recognition and playback run entirely on your device.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, only to read the sheet music in that image. The image is processed on your device by machine-learning models bundled with the app. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

There is no account or sign-in. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Crash reports

If you opt in to sharing diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us. We use them only to fix bugs.

TestFlight

When you test a beta build through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback and crash information you choose to send, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. Questions: yisheng.jiang@gmail.com.
