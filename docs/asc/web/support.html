AI Camera - Music Reader Support

AI Camera - Music Reader photographs printed sheet music, reads it on your device, and plays it back as MIDI with note highlights.

Contact

Email yisheng.jiang@gmail.com with your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and, if you can, a photo of the page that did not read correctly.

Tips for good results

  • Use clean, printed (engraved) sheet music. Handwritten music is not supported.
  • Photograph the page straight on, in even light, with the whole staff in frame.
